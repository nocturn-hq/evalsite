Answers before you ask.

How do I join?

Pick a username, use your Gmail, choose your country and set a strong password.

How much do I earn for each friend I invite?

You earn $0.62 for every friend who joins with your personal link. Your friend starts with a bonus too.

When do I get paid?

The next payout day is shown on your dashboard once it is announced. On that day, your payout link arrives in your email.

How are payments made?

All payments are made in US dollars through your bank, PayPal or Payoneer. Pick the one that works best for you when your payout link arrives.

Which countries can join?

Anyone, anywhere. Choose your country when you sign up and your balance shows a local currency estimate under the dollar amount.

Why do I need a Gmail address?

Your Gmail is how we keep one account per person and where your payout link is sent.

Can I log in with my username?

Yes. Log in with either your username or your Gmail address.

What makes a strong password?

At least 8 characters with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number and a symbol. You will see each rule tick off as you type.
